The Problem

Millions of neurodivergent individuals face delayed assessments, subjective evaluations, and reactive care models. Support often relies heavily on self-reporting or short clinical observations.

This leads to long wait times, inconsistent outcomes, and daily regulation challenges that go unaddressed between appointments.

The Solution

We are building an integrated approach to identify and support neurodivergent regulation patterns in a more objective and accessible way.

  • Support faster, more informed assessments
  • Provide ongoing daily regulation support
  • Reduce reliance on purely reactive care
  • Empower individuals with proactive tools

Our Vision

A future where neurodivergent individuals are supported with clarity, autonomy, and non-invasive solutions that work alongside existing care.
